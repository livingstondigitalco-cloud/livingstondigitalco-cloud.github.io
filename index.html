Your logo
Tips for a crisp engraving
  • SVG is best. A vector logo stays sharp at any size.
  • PNG or JPG? Use one at least 1000 pixels wide. A transparent background is ideal.
  • Use a one-color or high-contrast logo. Photos, gradients, and soft shadows don’t engrave cleanly.
  • Keep it simple. Very thin lines and tiny text can fill in on wood, so a simplified version of your logo works best.
  • Not sure? Send what you have. We’ll clean it up and email you a proof before anything is cut.
